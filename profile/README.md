**DY PROOF is independent technology diagnostics.** We determine where a running
system stops behaving acceptably — its operating limits and failure boundaries —
and prove it with evidence you can reproduce.

> Don't tell us it works. Let's DY it.

---

## Engagements

Each engagement answers one question, and leads to the next only if you need it.

| Engagement | The question it answers | Price |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Failure Boundary** · 72 hours | Where does one critical workflow stop behaving acceptably? | **$750** |
| **System Boundary Map** · 7 working days | How far can the whole system go, and what gives way first? | **$3,900** |
| **Runtime Diagnostics** · 2–3 weeks | Why does it break, and does the fix really move the boundary? | **from $9,500** |
| **Pre-investment examination** · private | Where does the target's system break, before capital depends on it? | scoped with the deal |

The 72-hour fee is credited in full toward the Boundary Map if you continue within

is agreed. **[Engagements →](https://dy-proof.github.io/#engagements)** ·

## What you receive

A **Failure Boundary Report**: the finding, the operating range, the boundary, the
mechanism behind it, the evidence, the conditions to reproduce it, the recommended
action and a stated confidence. Not a benchmark score, not an opinion, not pass or
fail. **[See the specimen →](https://dy-proof.github.io/#report)**

## DY PROOF or DY Research?

| | The question | The method | It ends in |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **DY PROOF** · Prove | Where does the running system stop working? | Produces new evidence: the system examined under controlled load | A documented, reproducible boundary |
| **DY Research** · Verify | Is what is claimed true? | Reads the evidence that exists: code, tests and proofs | A written verdict |

Different questions, different prices, one standard of evidence. For a deal, the two
run as one engagement: one scope, one report.

## Stated plainly

- **Not a certification.** No examination issues or implies qualification under any safety or compliance standard.
- **Not investment advice.** The findings are technical; the decision stays yours.
- **Not a warranty.** What you receive is evidence and reasoning, set out so that it can be checked.
- **Independent.** Belonging to the family never shapes a finding. If a system under examination competes with AxonOS or builds on it, you are told at scoping, before you commit to anything.
